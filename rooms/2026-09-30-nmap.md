## Summary
Intro to host discovery, port scanning, and service detection with Nmap.

## What I learned
- SYN scan (`-sS`) is half-open and needs root; TCP connect scan (`-sT`) completes the handshake
- `-sV` probes open ports to identify service versions
- `-p-` scans all 65535 ports instead of just the top 1000

## Commands / tools worth remembering
```bash
nmap -sC -sV -oN scan.txt <target-ip>
nmap -p- --min-rate 1000 <target-ip>
```

## What tripped me up
- Forgot `-Pn` when the host blocked ping, so Nmap reported it as down
